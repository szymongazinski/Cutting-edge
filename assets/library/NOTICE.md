# Cutting edge starter stickers

The 40 animated GIFs in the sticker folder are original procedural illustrations for Cutting edge. You may use, modify, and redistribute them in videos and other projects. The project authors dedicate these files to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The editable shapes, animation instructions, and color palettes are in scripts/build-stickers.cjs and app/synthesis.cjs.
