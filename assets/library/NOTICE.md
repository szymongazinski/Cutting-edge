# Cutting edge starter stickers

The animated arrows, circles and underlines in the sticker folder are original illustrations for Cutting edge. You may use, modify, and redistribute them in videos and other projects. The project authors dedicate these files to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The editable paths, animation timing and color palettes are in scripts/build-stickers.cjs.
