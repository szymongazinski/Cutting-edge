# Third-party components

The Cutting edge application code uses the MIT license. Dependencies retain their licenses.

- Electron and Chromium ship their license files with the Windows runtime.
- The FFmpeg and FFprobe executables ship under the GPL with their license and build/source links in resources/tools. See [media tools](docs/media-tools.md).
- GIF encoding uses gifenc (MIT). Its distribution includes its license in node_modules/gifenc.
- Interface icons come from Lucide. Its ISC/MIT terms are included in assets/icons/LICENSE.
- Inter and Barlow Condensed use the SIL Open Font License. Both notices are included under assets/fonts.
- ListenActive and its upstream speech models retain their own licensing. The optional runtime is obtained from the ListenActive release, and model weights use ListenActive's local cache.
- The initial sticker families are generated from this repository's own animation code. See [asset notice](assets/library/NOTICE.md). The Alpine sample uses an original AI-generated landscape with two camera moves and synthesized ambience. The sample media may be reused with this editor.
- The assistant can download media at the user's request. Media acquired from Wikimedia Commons retain their individual source and license information alongside the downloaded files.
